# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a respectful, welcoming environment for everyone.

Treat others with kindness and professionalism. Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or the sharing of private information without permission.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately through the repository's security-reporting channel. Reports will be handled promptly and respectfully.

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or participants that violate this code of conduct.
